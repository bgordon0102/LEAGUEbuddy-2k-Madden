About Tyrese Martin

Tyrese Martin is a 26-year-old American professional basketball player who plays at the Shooting Guard or Point Guard position for the Brooklyn Nets in the NBA. He is 6 feet 6 inches (198cm) tall, weighs 215 pounds (97kg), and has a wingspan of 6 feet 10 inches (208cm).

Martin played college basketball for two seasons at the University of Rhode Island and two seasons at the University of Connecticut. He joined the UConn Huskies for the 2020-2021 season and played through the 2021-2022 season before declaring for the NBA Draft. He was selected as the 51st overall pick by the Golden State Warriors in the 2022 NBA Draft.

For the 2025-26 NBA season, Tyrese Martin's base salary is $2,191,897.

Tyrese Martin on NBA 2K26

On NBA 2K26, the Current Version of Tyrese Martin has an Overall 2K Rating of 74 with a Point-Fading Wizard Build. He has a total of 4 Badges. The best aspect of Martin's game on 2K is his Outside Scoring. With a respectable 80 Mid-Range Shot Rating, he scores efficiently when shooting the ball from the perimeter. Additionally, his respectable 76 Three-Point Shot Rating makes him a threat when left wide open behind the three-point line.
